NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Earth analogs

Click any spot on Mars. Meet its twin on Earth.

3D Mars terrain, a ranked Earth analog for every point, and explained routes for the rover that fits the journey.

TerraTwin (Mars 3D) and Meridian (Earth geospatial AI) are one click apart.

Choosing an Earth analog is still guesswork

Data lives in different portals, matches come without reasons, and site choice is separate from route and rover planning.

01

Earth Analog Finder

Pick a point on Mars and get the best Earth twin with the reasons shown. Matches use literature tags for landforms, minerals and habitability, plus environment levels that are labelled as assumptions, not measurements. 23 Earth sites, 8 Mars targets.

02

What Earth cannot reproduce

Every twin shows its limits: gravity (3.71 vs 9.81 m/s²), surface pressure (about 6 vs 1,013 mbar) and radiation measured by Curiosity RAD. Earth climate for each twin comes from NASA POWER.

03

Three routes, explained

Safest, Science and Direct routes between two points. Each says why it is safe or dangerous for the chosen rover, the best rover for the drawn route is ranked, and the rover drives A to B on the 3D globe.

How it works

  1. Click a point on the 3D Mars globe
  2. Get the best Earth twin, with reasons
  3. Compare three explained routes
  4. Watch the best rover drive A to B

The Mars 3D and Back to Earth buttons switch between TerraTwin and the Meridian Earth map. A dark mode and a Claude assistant (server side API key) are built in.
