Scientific Provenance & Verification Standard

ARES 3.0 strictly classifies data into Measured Observations (NASA PDS LOLA MEGT90N000EB elevation and MSL Curiosity RAD Level 2 dosimetry), Derived Scientific Quantities (local slopes, 1° relief, transects), and Physical Model Assumptions (Beer–Lambert solar flux, barometric air pressure).

All telemetry pipelines are verifiable against public NASA GSFC, PDS Geosciences, and Moon Trek open data endpoints.
