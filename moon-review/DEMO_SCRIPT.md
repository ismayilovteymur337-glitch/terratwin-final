# 90-second ARES demo

0–10 s: Open Mission Overview. “ARES is an educational Moon planning workspace. Every view states what is measured, modeled or assumed.”

10–25 s: Open the whole 3D globe. Rotate and zoom. Point out the LOLA topography source and the separate illustrative radiation particles.

25–40 s: Select Jezero, then Gale, and open regional terrain. “These are NASA landing reference coordinates. Terrain uses a coarse global elevation grid, so small hazards are unresolved.”

40–55 s: Open Radiation Explorer. Switch to Measurements and move Observation time across a flagged record and the overnight gap. “These are actual RAD Level 2 Detector E records at Gale context; the color flow is only visual emphasis.”

55–70 s: Enable Viking imagery. Point to the Moon Trek status and opacity control. Toggle it off to show the LOLA fallback.

70–82 s: Open Landing Site Analysis. Change safety weight; ranking updates. “Safety uses derived coarse slope and relief with explicit assumed thresholds. Other factors are assumptions.”

82–90 s: Open Data Sources & Methods and export the summary. “ARES exposes provenance and missing data so exploration stays honest.”
